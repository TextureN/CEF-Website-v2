About CEF

Development is more than funding. It is a pathway.

Community Enterprise Fund is a developmental credit and enterprise-support organisation focused on widening economic participation. We bring together finance, enterprise development, skills development and programme implementation.

We believe lasting impact comes from practical support, strong partnerships and a clear focus on the people and businesses that drive local economies.

  • Purpose-led finance and enterprise support
  • Skills development and agricultural pathways
  • Programme implementation and accountability

How we work

A practical journey from potential to progress.

Every initiative needs a clear pathway, the right partners and an understanding of what success should look like.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Listen & assess

Understand the need, the context and the people the work is intended to serve.

02 / DESIGN

Build the pathway

Align objectives, resources, partners and measurable outcomes.

03 / DELIVER

Implement together

Coordinate resources, support participants and focus on outcomes.

04 / LEARN

Measure & improve

Track progress, document evidence and strengthen future delivery.
