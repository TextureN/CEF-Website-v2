About CEF

Development is more than funding. It is a pathway.

Community Enterprise Fund (CEF) is a developmental credit and enterprise-support organisation focused on widening economic participation. Our work brings together finance, enterprise development, skills development and programme delivery.

We believe lasting impact comes from practical support, strong partnerships and a clear focus on the people and businesses that drive local economies.

How we work

A practical journey from potential to progress.

Every initiative needs a clear pathway, the right partners and an understanding of what success should look like.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Listen & assess

Understand the need, the context and the people the work is intended to serve.

02 / DESIGN

Build the pathway

Align finance, skills, delivery capacity, partners and measurable objectives.

03 / DELIVER

Implement together

Coordinate resources, support participants and keep delivery focused on outcomes.

04 / LEARN

Measure & improve

Track progress, document evidence and use learning to strengthen future impact.
