How we collaborate

Build a delivery model that works in practice.

We start by understanding the intended outcomes and the operating context, then work with partners to clarify the delivery model, capabilities, governance and reporting expectations.

  • Define the opportunity and the intended impact
  • Map roles, capacity, resources and delivery risks
  • Agree monitoring, reporting and communication
  • Review performance and improve collaboratively
