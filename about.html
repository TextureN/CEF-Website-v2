Who we are

Practical development, grounded in real opportunity.

CEF is a developmental credit and enterprise-support organisation. Our work is focused on helping small businesses pursue opportunities, supporting people to build practical capabilities, and working with partners to implement development initiatives.

We take an integrated view: access to finance matters, but so do operational capacity, market access, skills, reliable delivery and accountability.

  • Development finance and purchase order funding experience
  • Enterprise development and business capability support
  • Skills development and agricultural training pathways
  • Programme coordination, monitoring and implementation
